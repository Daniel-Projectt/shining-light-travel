Meet Misty Vergakis

A Passion for Places
That Change You

Growing up in a Navy family and moving frequently from a young age instilled in me a deep appreciation for travel and the meaningful experiences it creates. I have had the privilege of living in beautiful places around the world, from San Diego to the Philippines to Florida. For the past 25 years, South Carolina has been home, while my passion for discovering new destinations and creating memorable journeys has continued to grow.

I believe the best gifts are experiences, and planning exceptional vacations is truly what I love. I specialize in luxury travel for families and couples, designing personalized getaways that create lasting memories.
